# Patent non-assertion pledge

Asimovia, publisher of DNApps through the MeshKore project, promises not to
assert any patent claim it owns or controls, now or in the future, against
anyone for making, using, selling or distributing an implementation of the
DNApps specification, or for writing, building or sharing DNApps capsules.

This pledge covers every version of the specification published in this
repository. It is irrevocable, except toward a party that asserts a patent
claim against Asimovia, or against any implementer, over an implementation of
DNApps. In that case the pledge ends for that party only.

This pledge is in addition to the patent license in Apache-2.0, which covers
the schema.
